This project is a Figma-like visual product page builder for e-commerce, built with Gradio and PyTorch. It allows users to drag, position, and resize blocks such as the product image, title, price, description, and call-to-action button on a canvas, while a small pretrained CNN analyzes the uploaded product image to suggest color palettes or layout templates. Users can override any suggestion, save or load their design as JSON, and export the final composed page as a PNG image.
